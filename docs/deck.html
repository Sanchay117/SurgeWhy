02 · Track 2 Explainability Standard

Four Auditable Pillars in Every Decision

1. Primary-Key Causal Chain

Traces every step from Carrier Claim -> NOAA METAR -> Tail MEL/Crew Log -> Statutory Clause with clickable [schema.table#id] citations.

2. Ruled-Out Hypotheses Ledger

Explicitly lists competing defenses tested and falsified (local weather, ATC ground stop, inbound bird strike) or honestly upheld.

3. Live Counterfactual Flip Lab

Computes the exact minimum change in arrival minutes, Ground Stop status, or maintenance minutes that flips OVERTURNED to DENIED.

4. Calibrated Confidence & Abstention

When sensor uncertainty (+/-7m cabin door-open gap on LH-455) straddles the 180m legal cliff, SurgeWhy pauses at 62% confidence and asks for 1 photo.
